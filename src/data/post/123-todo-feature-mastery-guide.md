## Unlocking the Full Potential of 123 To Do

Once you've mastered the foundational **1-2-3 Rule** (1 High priority task, 2 Medium tasks, and 3 Low tasks), you quickly discover that **123 To Do** is far more than a basic checklist. Underneath its deceptively clean and calm surface lies a suite of thoughtfully engineered productivity tools designed for professionals, deep-work thinkers, and anyone who wants to conquer their day without digital clutter.

To help you get the most out of your workflow without feeling overwhelmed, we’ve structured this guide by **perceived value and impact**—starting with the game-changing daily routines and moving into advanced power tools.

Here is your master walkthrough.

---

## 1. Midnight Rollover & The Daily Fresh Slate (Daily Impact: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)

The biggest failure mode of traditional to-do lists is **task debt**. When you end a Monday with 14 unfinished tasks in a giant backlog, Tuesday starts with dread before you’ve even had your morning coffee.

### How Rollover Works in 123 To Do

- **Automatic Midnight Reset**: When midnight arrives in your local time zone, completed tasks are gracefully archived into your history log, freeing up your daily quota.
- **Unfinished Tasks Carry Over**: Any incomplete tasks gently roll over into the new day. You don't have to re-enter them or feel guilty—they simply remain in their priority tier so you can tackle them first.
- **Zero-Friction Review**: In the morning, you open 123 To Do to find a clean slate with your carried-over priorities ready for review. You can re-prioritize or reassign them with a single swipe or drag.
- **Streak & Consistency Counter**: Every day you complete your core tasks, your streak increments. Consistency beats intensity every single time.

---

## 2. The Analog Clock Face & Calendar Time-Blocking (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)

A list of tasks tells you _what_ to do. It doesn't tell you _when_ you are actually going to do it.

123 To Do features an interactive **Analog Clock Face** visualizer that bridges the gap between task management and calendar scheduling.

### How to Use the Clock Face

1. **Assign a Time Slot**: When creating or editing any task, tap the clock icon to assign a start time and estimated duration (e.g., 9:00 AM – 10:30 AM).
2. **Visual Chronological Ring**: The Clock Face plots your day around an intuitive 12-hour or 24-hour circular dial. Tasks appear as color-coded wedges matching their priority (Red = Priority 1, Orange = Priority 2, Blue = Priority 3).
3. **Instant Gap Detection**: You can immediately see dead time, buffer windows, and whether you’ve accidentally scheduled two deep-work tasks at the exact same hour.
4. **Calendar Sync (.ICS Export)**: Prefer having your 123 To Do schedule in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook? You can export your daily schedule or sync your tasks with a single tap.

---

## 3. Subtask Checklists & Granular Progress (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐⭐)

A single priority task (like _"Finalize quarterly client proposal"_) can often feel intimidating. When a task feels too big, our brains naturally procrastinate.

### Breaking Tasks into Actionable Micro-Steps

- **Add Subtasks in Seconds**: Tap any task card to expand its detail modal and add sequential subtasks.
- **Visual Progress Ring**: Each task card displays a real-time progress indicator (e.g., `3/5` or a radial completion ring) directly on your home screen.
- **Milestone Momentum**: Checking off subtasks gives you immediate dopamine hits, keeping your focus locked on the objective until the overarching priority is complete.

---

## 4. Hands-Free Voice Dictation & Fast Capture (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐⭐)

Great ideas and sudden reminders rarely arrive while you’re sitting comfortably at your desk. They strike when you’re walking the dog, commuting, or cooking dinner.

### Instant Voice Input

- **One-Tap Dictation**: Tap the microphone icon beside the task input field on mobile or desktop.
- **Natural Speech Processing**: Speak naturally: _"Draft the contract addendum for Sarah by three o'clock"_.
- **Whisper-Fast Processing**: The transcription engine captures your words instantly, allowing you to get thoughts out of your head and into your organized system in seconds without typing a single letter.

---

## 5. Visual Attachments: Photos, Receipts & Screenshots (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐⭐)

Sometimes words alone aren't enough to capture the context of a task.

### Why Visual Context Matters

- **Attach Photos & Screenshots**: Upload whiteboard photos, receipt images, wireframes, or reference documents directly to any task card.
- **Offline & On-Device Storage**: Unlike cloud services that compress your files or charge you monthly storage tiers, your attachments are stored locally within your browser's persistent IndexedDB storage.
- **Instant Preview**: Tap any task to inspect high-resolution images immediately, perfect for shopping lists, contractor specs, or design reviews.

## 6. Dual Visual Themes: Retro Palm vs. Modern Slate (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐)

Productivity is personal, and the environment you work in shapes your focus. 123 To Do ships with two meticulously crafted UI personalities:

### 1. Modern Slate (OLED Dark & High-Contrast Light)

- Clean, contemporary typography with deep blacks and crisp neutral borders.
- Ideal for modern MacBook, iPad, and high-DPI smartphone displays.
- Built for minimal eye strain during late-night work sessions.

### 2. The Retro Palm LCD Theme

- An authentic tribute to early PDA pioneers (such as the Palm Pilot and classic green LCD monitors).
- Featuring authentic phosphor-green hues, dot-matrix styling, and tactile button feedback.
- Loved by minimalist purists who want to eliminate all modern digital gloss and focus entirely on raw text.

_Switch between them at any time in **Settings ⚙️ ➔ Theme**._

## 7. Projects, Tags & Kanban Board Views (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐)

While your daily view is strictly limited to 6 core tasks (1-2-3), real life requires tracking ongoing initiatives that span across weeks or months.

### Managing Complex Projects

- **Custom Projects**: Group tasks under custom color-coded projects (e.g., `#Work`, `#Health`, `#SideProject`, `#Home`).
- **Kanban Board Visualization**: Switch from the priority list to an intuitive multi-column Kanban board (`To Do`, `In Progress`, `Review`, `Done`).
- **Drag & Drop Reordering**: Seamlessly move tasks between stages on desktop or touchscreens, making it effortless to run sprint planning or manage multi-stage deliverables.

---

## 8. Zero-Knowledge Multi-Device Sync (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐)

Want to draft your tasks on your Mac or PC in the morning and check them off on your iPhone or Android during the day?

### How 123 To Do Syncs Without Storing Your Data

- **End-to-End Client-Side Encryption (AES-256-GCM)**: Before your task data leaves your device, it is encrypted using your private passphrase or secure room pairing code.
- **Zero-Knowledge Architecture**: The synchronization relay never sees your task names, notes, or subtasks. Even if someone intercepted the transmission, it is mathematically unreadable gibberish.
- **Pair in 10 Seconds**: Open **Settings ➔ Sync**, generate your 6-digit sync code or QR code on your first device, scan or enter it on your second device, and your lists stay in real-time harmony.

---

## 9. Automated Shadow Backups & Zero Lock-In (Impact: ⭐⭐⭐)

We believe you should own your data forever. You should never be locked into a proprietary ecosystem or lose work because of a browser cache clear.

### Safety Nets Built In

- **24-Hour Shadow Backups**: 123 To Do automatically creates rolling snapshot backups every day in isolated persistent local storage. If you ever make a mistake or accidentally delete a card, you can restore from yesterday's snapshot with a single click.
- **One-Click JSON & CSV Export**: Export your complete database at any time.
- **Todoist & Task Importer**: Moving from Todoist or another task manager? Paste or upload your tasks, and 123 To Do will automatically map them into your priorities.

---

## 10. Built-in Pomodoro Focus Timer & Velocity Stats (Impact: ⭐⭐)

When it’s time to stop organizing and actually execute, 123 To Do transforms into a dedicated deep-work timer.

### Deep Work Mode

- **Integrated Focus Timer**: Tap the timer on any active task to launch a 25-minute Pomodoro focus block.
- **Ambient Focus Audio**: Optional subtle ambient white noise / focus audio to block out distractions.
- **Productivity Velocity**: Track how many tasks you complete per week, identify peak focus hours, and celebrate your wins with zero vanity metrics.
