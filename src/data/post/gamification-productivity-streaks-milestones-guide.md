## 🧠 The Neuroscience of Productivity Points: Why Gamified Focus Works

Human brains are hardwired for immediate feedback loops. When you complete an action and receive clear visual recognition, your brain releases a micro-dose of dopamine — the neurotransmitter responsible for motivation, habit formation, and goal pursuit.

In 123 ToDo, every productive action earns **Productivity Points**:

- 📋 **+10 Points** per completed task archived
- 📌 **+2 Points** per checklist subtask step checked off
- 🔥 **+15 Points** per consecutive daily streak day maintained

Because points are earned through real-world execution rather than vanity metrics, your score directly reflects your personal productivity output.

## 🎯 How the 1-2-3 Rule Powers Your Score

Most people fail with to-do lists because they treat all tasks equally. A 2-minute email gets the same weight as writing a comprehensive quarterly report.

The **1-2-3 Rule** eliminates this decision fatigue:

1. **1 Must-Do Task (P1)**: The single highest-leverage item. If you only finish this today, your day is a definitive victory.
2. **2 Should-Do Tasks (P2)**: High-value tasks that move major milestones forward.
3. **3 Could-Do Tasks (P3)**: Quick wins, chores, or secondary items if energy allows.

Inside the **Achievements & Productivity Modal**, the **1-2-3 Balance Insight** visualizes your execution ratio across Must, Should, and Could tasks, ensuring you stay focused on high-leverage outcomes rather than getting lost in low-value busywork.
