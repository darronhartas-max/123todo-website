## 1. The Real Cost of Lost Field Notes

Trade contractors, site supervisors, and field surveyors routinely encounter critical updates throughout the day:

- On-site measurements that need logging immediately.
- Unexpected safety or material shortages requiring follow-up orders.
- Client change requests requested verbally during walk-throughs.

When notes are jotted down on scrap paper or left unrecorded until the end of the day, key details get lost, leading to costly rework and missed project deadlines.
